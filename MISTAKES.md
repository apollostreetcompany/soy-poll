- Typeform's full-page embed can cover wrapper controls after hydration even
  while those controls remain in the accessibility tree. Inspect the settled
  screenshot and click the real privacy link. Relative layout containment on
  the existing embed keeps its positioned frame inside the survey section.

  than guessing a terms URL. The actual service-terms URL differs from the
  initially assumed path; it was corrected before publication.
